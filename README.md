# Hi, I'm Jessica

I work at the intersection of machine learning, empirical research, and market analysis.

My work focuses on building predictive systems that are not just accurate, but also statistically honest: calibrated intervals, leakage-aware pipelines, and evaluation designed to answer the real question.

### Currently exploring
- applied ML in high-noise, real-world settings
- financial and market research with strong statistical discipline
- robust evaluation, calibration, and uncertainty estimation
- reproducible research with transparent assumptions and audit trails

### Featured projects
- [art_price_predictor](https://github.com/w-jessica-wang/art_price_predictor)  
  An interpretable ensemble for fine-art auction price prediction with calibrated uncertainty intervals and walk-forward evaluation.

- [risk_vs_return](https://github.com/w-jessica-wang/risk_vs_return)  
  A pre-registered empirical study testing whether crypto carry is compensation for crash risk, with measured trading costs and a sealed holdout.
